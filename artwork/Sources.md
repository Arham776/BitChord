# BitChord Apple cover

## Design assets used

- Original app icon and three screenshots: the user’s Downloads folder, October 1, 2026.
- Native Mac window corners and shadows: `Windows/Dark/Window BG (with Sidebar)/Active`, from the installed Apple macOS 27 UI Kit. The original screenshot supplies the window’s interface and controls; only its surrounding black margin is cropped.
- iPhone frame: Hoverify’s independently drawn iPhone 17 Pro Max, Deep Blue. https://tryhoverify.com/tools/device-frames/iphone-17-pro-max/
- iPad frame: Hoverify’s independently drawn iPad Pro 11 M5, Space Black, rotated into landscape. https://tryhoverify.com/tools/device-frames/ipad-pro-11-m5/

Hoverify permits these frames in personal and commercial mockups. Its frame files may not be redistributed as a frame pack or mockup tool. Keep them as assets for this cover. https://tryhoverify.com/tools/device-frames/

## Editable structure

`BitChord-Apple-Cover.sketch` contains editable text, native gradient lights, the rounded icon mask, native window effects and separate original screenshot/device bezel layers. Feature text sits directly on the background, without a container. Device screenshots retain their original aspect ratios. The artboard crops the foreground devices at the bottom, following the supplied reference’s overlapping composition.

The Apple product bezel disk images were downloaded but not opened or used; they require a separate license acceptance. This cover uses the independently drawn Hoverify frames instead.
